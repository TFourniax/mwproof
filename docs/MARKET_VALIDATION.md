# Market validation — 18 Aug 2026

## Demand exists

The European market is already capital-intensive and supply-constrained. CBRE's 2026 European outlook forecasts data-centre vacancy falling to 6.5%, more than 750 MW of new capacity in Europe during 2026, and explicitly says grid bottlenecks are extending project lead times. CBRE also notes that newer GPUaaS tenants can have limited operating histories, increasing the importance of risk evaluation.

European lenders are not withdrawing: CBRE's 2026 lender survey covers 134 lenders and about €70bn of expected origination; 72% intend to increase origination, 69% are willing to underwrite development loans, and median LTV for prime data centres increased year-on-year.

Concrete European financings are large: Mistral announced $830m of debt for its French AI infrastructure; Pure DC announced €1.3bn of senior debt for the first phase of its Finland AI campus. France is pursuing multi-GW AI-campus capacity.

Insurance/risk demand is also demonstrably expanding: Aon launched/expanded a data-centre lifecycle programme with up to $5bn capacity in July 2026; Swiss Re expects global data-centre insurance premiums to rise from about $10.6bn to $24.2bn by 2030.

## What is not yet validated

We have not yet proven willingness to pay specifically for `MW@Confidence`. The commercial falsification test is 10-15 conversations with European infrastructure lenders, private-credit teams, insurers/reinsurers and developers, asking them to underwrite a historical deal with and without ProofMW and measuring whether pricing, covenants, diligence time or go/no-go changes.
